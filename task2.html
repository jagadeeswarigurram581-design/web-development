Incredible India - Travel Tips & Best Destinations

1. Historical Monuments (By Region)

Taj Mahal - North

Type: Historical Monument

Located in Agra, symbol of love, built by Shah Jahan.

Hampi - South

Type: Heritage Site

Ancient Vijayanagara ruins in Karnataka.

Konark Sun Temple - East

Type: Temple Heritage

Famous chariot temple in Odisha.

2. Cultural Festivals

Pongal - Tamil Nadu

Harvest festival, Jan 14, Sweet Pongal dish.

Durga Puja - West Bengal

Navratri festival, Goddess Durga worship.

Hornbill - Nagaland

Festival of festivals, tribal dance & music.

3. Famous Destinations by Attraction Type

  • Beach Type: Goa, Puri, Kovalam
  • Hill Type: Manali, Ooty, Darjeeling
  • Desert Type: Jaisalmer, Rann of Kutch
  • Spiritual Type: Varanasi, Tirupati, Amritsar

  • Carry ID proof for monuments
